PEACE OF MIND FOR FAMILIES

Why Modern Subdivisions Choose C.L.E.V.A.

Eliminating gasoline fumes, loud revving engines, and driveway congestion with purpose-engineered neighborhood electric vehicles.

Silent 24/7 Security Patrols

Security guards can conduct perimeter checks at 2 AM without awakening sleeping families or barking dogs. Officers have full sensory awareness to hear subtle surroundings.

• Zero Engine Vibration & Noise

Gate-to-Door Feeder Shuttles

Provide reliable, air-conditioned or breeze-ventilated transit from the main guard gate to inner cul-de-sacs. Ideal for elderly residents, school children, and household staff.

• Eliminates Long Walks with Groceries

Safe Speed-Governed Drivetrains

Programmable speed limiters (e.g. 20-30 kph) prevent reckless speeding inside residential streets where children play on sidewalks and bikes.

• High Visibility & Automatic Braking

Shared Community EV Pods

HOAs can implement community-shared compact EVs at the clubhouse for residents to borrow for quick market trips, reducing the need for second and third household cars.

• Decongests Narrow Residential Curbs

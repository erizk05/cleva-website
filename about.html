WHO IS C.L.E.V.A.

C.L.E.V.A. is a forward-thinking electric vehicle brand on a mission to modernize and democratize passenger transport. We are driven by the belief that sustainable mobility should be accessible, reliable, and intelligently designed for everyone.

Founded by transport engineers and clean energy advocates, we design, engineer, and support a comprehensive range of electric passenger vehicles. Our focus goes beyond manufacturing; we create end-to-end ecosystems that integrate vehicles, infrastructure, and support services to deliver seamless transit experiences.

WHY C.L.E.V.A. STANDS OUT

What sets C.L.E.V.A. apart is our uncompromising dedication to purpose-built design and comprehensive ecosystem support. We do not offer one-size-fits-all solutions; we engineer vehicles tailored to specific regional challenges and operational realities.

Purpose-Built Engineering

Our vehicles are crafted to endure demanding terrains, varied climates, and intense daily usage, ensuring unmatched durability and performance.

Economic Viability

We drastically reduce total cost of ownership by maximizing energy efficiency and minimizing maintenance downtime, making green transit economically smart.

End-to-End Partnership

From initial route planning to driver training and proactive maintenance, we stand beside operators to guarantee continuous, frictionless service.

EMPOWERING COMMUNITIES

Transitioning to electric mobility is more than an environmental imperative; it is a catalyst for community growth. At C.L.E.V.A., we empower communities by providing cleaner air, quieter streets, and equitable transport solutions.

  • Creating local green jobs in vehicle assembly, maintenance, and fleet operation.
  • Reducing localized pollution, leading to improved public health outcomes.
  • Fostering economic independence by lowering transit operational costs for drivers.

SERVICING MARGINALISED COMMUNITIES

Mobility is a fundamental human right. Too often, marginalized communities are left disconnected by traditional transit networks. C.L.E.V.A. is committed to bridging this gap.

We design adaptable vehicles, such as our agile 3-wheelers, to navigate narrow streets and underserved routes safely and efficiently. By partnering with local governments and cooperatives, we ensure that clean, affordable transportation reaches those who need it most, driving inclusive growth and breaking down barriers to opportunity.

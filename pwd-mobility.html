DESIGNED FROM THE GROUND UP

Engineering Features That Eliminate Barriers

Every accessible C.L.E.V.A. vehicle is built in compliance with international barrier-free design standards and national accessibility laws (BP 344).

Effortless Roll-On / Roll-Off Ramps

Lightweight, high-strength aluminum ramps with gentle 1:6 inclines. Non-slip diamond surfaces ensure confident entry for motorized wheelchairs and manual chairs alike without lifting.

• Zero Lifting Required | Anti-Slip Traction

Certified 4-Point Docking & Belts

Industrial-grade floor-mounted anchor tracks with automatic self-tensioning retractors and integrated 3-point occupant lap and shoulder seatbelts to protect wheelchair passengers during transit.

• High-Impact Crash Tested Securement

PWD Driver Livelihood Controls

Adapted steering spinners, electronic hand throttles, and push-pull hand braking mechanisms allow drivers with lower-limb impairments to operate independently and earn livelihoods as transport entrepreneurs.

• Independent Earning & Self-Sufficiency

Dialysis & Medical Transit Comfort

Pneumatic and multi-link shock absorption cushions road irregularities, providing ultra-gentle transport for patients recovering from dialysis, chemotherapy, or physical rehabilitation.

• Low-Floor Step-In & High-Ceiling Clearance

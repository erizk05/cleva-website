BATTERY ELECTRIC MOBILITY

At CLEVA, we leverage cutting-edge technology to create electric vehicles that are efficient, reliable, and sustainable.

DIRECT BATTERY MOBILITY

Our vehicles utilize state-of-the-art battery systems designed for direct electric mobility, offering superior safety, extended lifespans, and reliable zero-emission performance without reliance on fossil fuels.

DIRECT GENERATOR MOBILITY (NO BATTERY CHARGING)

Our direct generator mobility solutions provide continuous electric drive without the need for external plug-in battery charging, ensuring uninterrupted service for demanding operational environments.

RANGE EXTENDERS (EREV)

Our Extended-Range Electric Vehicle (EREV) architecture combines the benefits of electric propulsion with an onboard generator to extend operational distances and eliminate range anxiety.

REGENERATIVE BRAKING

Our intelligent braking system captures kinetic energy typically lost during deceleration and feeds it back into the battery, maximizing efficiency in stop-and-go transit routes.
